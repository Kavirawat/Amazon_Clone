# Amazon Clone (Glassmorphism Edition)

A modern, ultra-sleek Amazon Clone web application featuring a stunning **Glassmorphism UI**, an **automatic 5-slide Hero Carousel**, and full product catalogs across all categories.

## Features

- **Automatic 5-Slide Hero Carousel**:
  - 🔄 **Auto-slides every 4.5 seconds** with smooth cross-fade zoom animation.
  - ⏸️ **Auto-pauses on hover** so users can comfortably read and click call-to-action buttons.
  - ◀️ ▶️ **Glassmorphism Prev & Next navigation buttons**.
  - 🔘 **5 Interactive Dot Indicators** with active gold pill highlight.
  - 🌟 **5 Themed Hero Banners**:
    1. **Big Billion Savings Days**: Up to 50% off mega deals & shopping packages.
    2. **Next-Gen Tech & Gaming**: Flagship iPhone 15 Pro, S24 Ultra & M3 MacBooks.
    3. **The Great Fashion Festival**: Levi's, Ralph Lauren, Nike sneakers & luxury bags up to 60% off.
    4. **Smart Home & Kitchen Makeover**: Ninja blenders, Instant Pots, air fryers & modern furniture.
    5. **Luxury Beauty & Fragrances**: Chanel Coco Noir, Dior Sauvage, MAC Lipsticks & CeraVe.
- **Filled Category Navigation Line (Starting with Home)**:
  - 🏠 **Home**: 16-item showcase spanning all product departments.
  - ⚡ **Today's Deals**: Up to 50% off lightning deals with deal tags.
  - 📱 **Mobiles**: iPhone 15 Pro Max, Galaxy S24 Ultra, Pixel 8 Pro, OnePlus 12, Xiaomi 14, Nothing Phone (2), etc.
  - 💻 **Laptops**: MacBook Pro M3 Max, Dell XPS 15 OLED, ROG Zephyrus, HP Spectre x360, ThinkPad X1, etc.
  - 🎧 **Electronics**: Sony WH-1000XM5, AirPods Max, Apple Watch Series 9, JBL Flip 6, Odyssey Neo G8, etc.
  - 👕 **Fashion**: Levi's Trucker Denim, Ralph Lauren Oxford Shirt, Nike Air Force 1, Fossil Watch, Ray-Ban Aviators, etc.
  - ✨ **Beauty**: Lash Princess Mascara, Urban Decay Naked3, MAC Velvet Teddy, Clinique 100H, CeraVe, etc.
  - 🍳 **Kitchen**: Ninja 1400W Blender, Cuisinart Cookware, Instant Pot Duo, Cosori Air Fryer XL, Wüsthof Knives, etc.
  - 🛋️ **Furniture**: Herman Miller Aeron, West Elm Coffee Table, Casper Mattress, Article Leather Sofa, Standing Desk, etc.
  - 🌸 **Fragrances**: Chanel Coco Noir, Dior Sauvage, YSL Black Opium, Tom Ford Tobacco Vanille, Creed Aventus, etc.
  - 🛒 **Groceries**: California Almonds, Raw Honey, Lavazza Coffee, Colavita Olive Oil, Twinings Tea, etc.
  - 🎧 **Customer Service**: Interactive customer support hub with live chat simulation, order tracking, and refund requests.
  - 🎁 **Gift Cards**: Amazon eGift Cards ($25, $50, $100, $250) ready to add to cart or redeem.
  - 🏪 **Sell**: "Sell on Amazon" portal with interactive product creation form to list items live.
- **Full Glassmorphism UI**:
  - Translucent frosted glass surfaces with `backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(180%)`.
  - Ambient mesh gradients, glowing highlights, floating product cards, and glass modals.
- **User Profile Menu (After Cart)**:
  - Theme switcher (Light ☀️, Dark 🌙, Midnight ✨).
  - Currency switcher with live conversion: **INR (₹)**, **USD ($)**, **EUR (€)**.
  - Location selector (India, USA, UK, Germany) synced with navbar.
  - Audio sound effects toggle with synthesized Amazon chimes.
  - Your Orders modal & tracking.
  - Your Wishlist modal & 1-click cart move.
  - Account settings & Profile edit.
- **Slide-out Cart Drawer**:
  - Quantity controls (`+` / `-`), item removal, and live order placement.
